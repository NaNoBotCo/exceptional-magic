THE PAPER THIS SITE READS
    A. Garrett Lisi, "Division Algebras, Triality, and Exceptional Magic",
    arXiv:2609.12112v1 [math-ph], 10 September 2026 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12112
    Licence: CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. That licence permits no derivative works, and this site is not
    one: no text, equation image, table image or figure from the paper is reproduced. The
    mathematics — division algebras, triality, the magic square, the exceptional root
    systems — is recomputed here from the definitions. The multiplication tables in
    tools/algebra.py are the standard ones, transcribed from the paper's §2 and then verified
    against the algebraic laws they must satisfy. The paper is cited by section wherever it
    is the reason a topic appears, and its 34 references are transcribed with their links on
    the credit page.
